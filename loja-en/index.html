Does it work on cat hair too?

Yes. It works on dog and cat hair on fabric surfaces with some texture, like sofas, blankets and carpets.

Will it damage my sofa?

It uses no glue or blades. On delicate fabrics, test it first on a hidden area. We do not recommend it for silk or fine knits.
